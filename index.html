The problem I solve

Programmes don’t fail on technology. They fail on architecture.

Cloud, AI and transformation programmes rarely fail because of the technology. They fail because the architecture cannot support the decisions being made — ownership fragments, governance lives in documents instead of operating practice, and delivery teams inherit a target no one can actually build. That is where I engage: decision rights defined, governance that operates in practice, business and technology strategy aligned, and an executable target architecture delivery teams can implement.

I don’t produce architectural presentations. I build architecture that enables execution.
