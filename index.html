Who I am

Phil Myint

I learned systems engineering in New Zealand by building systems, sharpened it on London’s trading floors — rooms where slow architecture costs real money by lunchtime — and now I write from Brisbane.

Three decades in, across more than fifty countries, I’ve seen most of the ways architecture fails and a few of the ways it wins. OrdoAnimi is where I write it down: the framework, the books, and the odd thing I build when an idea needs a working model more than another paragraph.

Architect by trade, author by habit, vibe coder when the mood takes me.
